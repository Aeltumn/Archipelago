# Rayman 2

## What are checks in this game?

The following things count as checks:
- Picking up Super Yellow Lums
- Destroying Cages
- Receiving Silver Lums
- Obtaining Masks
- Learning about the location of the Cave of Bad Dreams
- Obtaining the Elixir of Life

If lumsanity is enabled, all yellow lums become checks instead of just the super lums.

## What does randomization do to this game?

When the game is randomised sub-levels are shuffled around. Levels may be built up of any randomised sub levels inside. The contents of cages are not changed.

## I get errors when trying to generate this game, why?
Rayman 2 has lum gates which require progression items to be obtained consistently throughout many checks, this can make the game un-completable if too many cages or single lums are placed in early check locations. Due to the amount of checks Archipelago cannot guarantee it can move enough checks to make it fit. Lowering lum gate thresholds, disabling room randomisation, or adding other games to place checks in should increase the likelyhood the generation succeeds.
